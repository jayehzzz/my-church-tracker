# Unified charts and dashboards — 20 September 2026

Sunday Services’ Attendance Trend is the visual reference: quiet card borders, clear titles, primary/amber comparison colours, light gridlines, consistent line/bar controls, and compact metric summaries. Keep different visualisations when they answer different questions; a calendar, location map, attendance matrix, and participation radar should not become interchangeable line charts.

## Implemented locally

- Shared expanded chart modal preserves the existing chart instance and its comparison, grouping, selected metric and display mode. It moves outside clipping/animated ancestors, fills the phone viewport, manages keyboard focus and restores the opening control.
- Shared dialog stack coordinates chart expansion and nested details: only the top dialog handles Escape and Tab, background content is inert, scroll stays locked until the last dialog closes, and titles have unique accessible IDs.
- Expansion coverage: home attendance, Sunday attendance/mix/service mix/matrix, meeting analytics, outreach trend/outcomes, pastoral-care calendar, profile attendance rhythm, Development attendance/tithing/outreach, People locations, and the five report detail dashboards.
- Point details: attendance line/bar and grouped points; meeting line/bar and grouped points; outreach month summaries with contacts and nested profiles; calendar day records and individual care details; participation radar points; Development months and outreach bars; Sunday attendance mix and service mix. Existing person/meeting/segment drill-downs remain. Static KPI labels and navigation links are not turned into redundant single-number dialogs.
- Meeting time-series charts now use the shared geometry, curve and semantic palette. Outreach comparisons consistently use amber. Card headings and narrow layouts have been aligned. Phone charts avoid excessive SVG letterboxing; four-metric comparison footers use two columns on phones.
- Fixed hover crashes after grouping reduces the point count; aligned singleton series to calendar buckets; preserved raw meeting counts/averages when grouping; exposed an explicit “Show all” for meeting comparisons beyond eight types.
- Removed the Sunday trend’s overlapping raw-JSON Copy control and duplicate calendar detail heading/close control. CSV and other existing exports remain available.

## Recommended next simplifications

1. **One job per screen.** Home: what needs attention now. Section overview: trends and comparisons. Reports: period summaries and exports. Avoid rebuilding every section’s full dashboard in Reports.
2. **Reduce repeated statistics.** Sunday “At a glance” repeats highest attendance and recent decisions already shown elsewhere. Outreach summary and outcome cards repeat three outcome totals. Keep the actionable version and make secondary context collapsible.
3. **Keep three or four headline metrics.** Make additional metrics selectable rather than adding another permanent row. Show the period and denominator beside each metric; distinguish unique people, attendances and gatherings.
4. **Default to simple controls.** Show comparison and line/bar controls; place grouping and calculation controls under More options while preserving the user’s choice. Sunday’s full two-series controls remain available in this implementation.
5. **Add previous-period comparison deliberately.** Current comparisons mostly compare metrics, programmes or people. A separate previous-period/last-year mode would answer “are we improving?” It needs explicit equal-length/incomplete-period rules and adequate history; this is not implemented here.
6. **Avoid two competing participation dashboards.** Keep the profile radar as a brief snapshot, with a link to Development for programme selection, comparisons and reviews. Preserve the distinction between recorded weekly frequency and attendance against known registers.
7. **Retire unused chart implementations with their documentation — completed locally, 22 September 2026.** Removed `dashboard/AttendanceChart` and `charts/CategoryDonut`, `GrowthTimeline`, `LeaderHeatmap`, `MonthlyContactsBar`, `PrayerHoursChart`, `RoleDistribution` and `Sparkline` after checking static references, dynamic imports and glob loaders. Updated `docs/07-chart-components.md` to describe the retained components. This cleanup changes no schema or stored records.
8. **Prefer useful detail over decorative interactions.** Preserve meaningful breakdowns, dates, people and next actions. Do not add modals that only repeat a number or force a temporal comparison onto a calendar/map.

## A/B count and average comparisons — 20 September follow-up

- `ComparisonControls` is now the common two-series selector. A and B can use the same metric with independently selected actual-total or average calculations. `MetricComparison` provides the corresponding summary cards and detail modal for non-time-series dashboards. B can be disabled with None; selections survive fullscreen expansion.
- Active coverage: Home and Sunday AttendanceTrend; Sunday attendance/outcome summary; Meetings programme bars, time series and unique-person composition; Outreach monthly trend and outcome summary; Reports overview and all six section modes; care calendar; People locations summary; profile participation; Development attendance, recorded tithe dates and outreach. Maps, attendance matrices, percentage radars and record tables keep their specialist representations alongside the dashboard comparison controls. Unused legacy charts remain unchanged.
- Attendance/meeting time series calculate totals or per-gathering averages **within each week/month bucket**; Day shows individual recorded gatherings. Meeting programme bars use the programme's held-meeting count. Selecting the same programme twice does not duplicate its summary denominator.
- Outreach trends plot monthly actuals against a period monthly-average reference. Empty months and partial boundary months count; future months are excluded. Outreach outcome averages describe the cohort of contacts reached, not the dates on which later outcomes occurred. Actual month records are preserved for drill-down.
- Report averages use completed services, held meetings or calendar months as labelled. Care averages use elapsed calendar days in the intersection of selected dates and displayed month, including zero-activity days. Profile averages use its selected completed calendar weeks; Development uses offered weeks with known registers. Tithe-date averages use complete eligible months and respect confidential-data availability.
- Counts stay whole; calculated averages retain at most one decimal. Zero denominators and unavailable data show Unavailable. Unique people/current directory snapshots and open follow-ups have count-only comparisons. Decimal chart ticks remain distinct for small averages. Home, Sunday, Meetings and report attendance summaries use the same rounding convention.
- Replaced repeated Outreach outcome and meeting-composition tiles with selectable comparison cards, and suppress a duplicate Outreach average footer when A/B use the same metric. Decimal count-input validation from the parallel task remains intact.
- Verification: 277 unit tests across 52 files, production build, and diff whitespace check pass. Browser demo checks covered Outreach expansion and raw month details, Sunday total 403 versus average 134.3 and grouped modal, same-programme Meeting comparison, report total versus average, daily care average, Development comparison availability and a 390px report layout without page overflow.
